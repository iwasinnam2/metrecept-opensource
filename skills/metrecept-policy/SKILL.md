# Metrecept policy

Call `metrecept_policy()` for allowed web-fetch purposes
(`public_web_retrieval`, `business_catalog`, `public_company_info`,
`job_listings`) and their limits.

Requires Metrecept MCP (`METRECEPT_API_KEY`). Docs: https://metrecept.co.uk/docs/cursor
